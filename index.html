Fed & PolicyFederal Reserve Raises Rates to 3.75%-4.00%, First Hike in 3+ Years

The FOMC unanimously voted on September 16 to increase the federal funds rate by 25 basis points to 3.75%-4.00%, the first hike since 2023, as inflation remains elevated above the 2% target. Futures markets are pricing in another rate increase to 4.2% by December and 4.7% by September 2027. Markets initially sold off on the hike but recovered as oil prices fell on hopes for diplomatic progress in the Middle East.

Trade & GeopoliticsTrump-Xi Summit Set for Washington; US Delays Tariffs Pending Negotiations

President Xi Jinping is scheduled to arrive in Washington on September 24 for a state visit with President Trump, the first time in a decade. The summit will include bilateral meetings and a state dinner covering trade, tariffs, rare earth minerals, AI, and Taiwan. The US is delaying announcements of excess capacity tariffs (planned at 7.5% on Chinese goods) until after the summit, preserving them as negotiating leverage. Markets are optimistic that diplomatic engagement could ease trade tensions.

HealthcareNovo Nordisk Falls 6-7% After Unveiling $23 Billion Obesity Sales Target for 2030

Novo Nordisk (NVO) shares tumbled 6-7% following its Capital Markets Day presentation in London, where the company outlined an ambitious long-term strategy. The drugmaker set a target of over $23 billion in annual obesity sales by 2030 and plans to bring more than five blockbuster-potential medicines to market. Additionally, Novo's CagriSema combination therapy showed 21.0% weight loss versus 2.0% placebo in the REDEFINE 9 obesity trial, but investors remain concerned about intensifying competition in the crowded GLP-1 weight-loss market from companies like Eli Lilly and others.
